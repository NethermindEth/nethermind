## Pinned target

A proof instance is identified by all of:

- the EIP, execution-specification, Nethermind, and Lean revisions in [SPEC.md](SPEC.md);
- a mainnet chain-spec revision and exact fork activation block or timestamp;
- the resolved standard main-processing dependency graph;
- compiler, Roslyn extractor, build properties, preprocessor symbols, and generated-artifact hashes.

`SPEC.md` selects the standard mainnet processing graph with Amsterdam enabled at a synthetic genesis boundary. That is an exact experimental proof configuration, but it is not the currently scheduled live-mainnet configuration; reports must preserve that distinction until a real activation is pinned.

The semantic adapter from that synthetic timestamp-0/block-0 selection to the configured chain-spec/spec-provider and runtime standard-mainnet fork route remains open. Source and activation metadata alone do not discharge that adapter or establish a live-mainnet activation claim.

The target production graph is the standard mainnet path at the pinned Nethermind revision:

## Observable theorem

For a pinned configuration and related initial states, the Lean processor and the resolved production processor must agree on the consensus-visible observation:

```text
theorem standard_mainnet_processor_refines :
  TargetPinned target ->
  RepresentsInput productionInput leanInput ->
  RepresentsPreState productionState leanState ->
  observeProduction (runStandardMainnet productionInput productionState) =
    observeLean (runPinnedMainnet leanInput leanState)
```

The observation contains:

- block acceptance or rejection and the consensus error category;
- per-frame success, revert, or exceptional halt, returndata, logs, and durable state effects;
- account existence, nonce, balance, code, storage, original/current values, and warm account/key sets;
- execution gas, state reservoir, spill/refill, state gas used, refund counter, remaining gas, and frame entry/exit records;
- transaction acceptance, status, paid gas, execution/state gas, cumulative receipt gas, and receipt fields;
- final account/storage/code state, withdrawals and protocol-system effects, execution requests, block gas dimensions, bloom, receipts root, and state root.

Tracing events, log messages, metrics, allocation behavior, and wall-clock ordering are not observations unless they can alter a consensus-visible result.

## Trust boundary

The proof may rely on, but must name and version:

- the Lean kernel and compiler;
- the C# compiler's source semantics and Roslyn binding information;
- the extractor and its mapping from the accepted C# subset to the generated Lean definition;
- the theorem prover host, operating system, and hardware behaving according to their specifications.

The following may begin as explicit parameters, but are not silently assumed:

- Keccak and other cryptographic primitives;
- individual precompile functions;
- RLP, receipt encoding, Merkle Patricia trie updates, and root calculation;
- world-state read, write, snapshot, restore, and commit adapter laws;
- equivalence of parallel block execution to the verified sequential semantics.

A conditional theorem must expose these assumptions in its statement and claim text. State-map equivalence must not be reported as state-root verification. Differential agreement, tests, and mutation resistance support the extraction and adapter trust boundary; they are not substitutes for refinement proofs.

CLR/JIT/AOT correctness and database persistence or crash recovery are excluded even from the complete source-level theorem. The final claim must remain qualified as verification of the pinned standard mainnet execution pipeline under the published trust boundary.

## Claim gates

All gates are fail-closed:

1. **Identity:** every source, tool, chain-spec, fork activation, compilation option, and generated artifact is pinned and reproducible.
2. **Specification:** the complete Lean EVM, transaction, and block semantics build without `sorry`, `admit`, or undeclared axioms.
3. **Extraction:** every selected production root and transitive semantic dependency is accepted by the restricted extractor; unsupported constructs fail the build.
4. **Refinement:** checked theorems connect each generated kernel to its Lean specification and compose through opcode, frame, transaction, and block boundaries.
5. **Adapters:** world-state, stack, memory, access-tracker, journal, receipt, and root adapters have discharged simulation obligations or remain named theorem assumptions.
6. **Reachability:** the pinned production DI graph and opcode tables use the verified kernels on every target path, with no unverified semantic bypass.
7. **Execution modes:** traced/untraced and sequential/parallel target modes are proved observationally equivalent or explicitly removed from the target.
8. **Validation:** deterministic vectors, boundary/property tests, EEST/Hive replay, and mutation tests agree across Lean and Nethermind.
9. **Artifacts and performance:** regeneration is clean and cross-platform deterministic; warning-as-error builds pass; kernel integration has no unacceptable hot-path regression.
10. **Assumption audit:** every remaining trusted or parameterized component appears in the published theorem and claim, with no stronger wording than the discharged result permits.

Until all ten gates are green for the same target manifest, only component-level claims are allowed.
