# TLOAD/TSTORE extraction scope

This extractor closes the standard-mainnet Amsterdam route from `IVirtualMachine` registration through `EthereumVirtualMachine`, the eight exact per-opcode roots across all four tracing/cancellation tables, the tracing-specialized `TLoadOpcode` and non-generic `TStoreOpcode` wrappers, and their immediate instruction bodies. It admits the complete source bytes for that route and the full named-mainnet fork ancestry. Each complete-source admission has a unique path-qualified `compilation-unit/0` identity bound to its reviewed source fingerprint. Regeneration cannot update the reviewed admission hashes.

The serialized IR and source manifest are read back with case-sensitive property matching, unknown-member rejection, required-constructor enforcement, and explicit top-level and nested null rejection. Exact validation binds every header, reachability, opcode, effect-order, specialization-root and obligation field before Lean emission. The manifest independently binds all source/admission identities, source and artifact fingerprints, combined fingerprint and semantic-binding statements. Adversarial tests cover malformed, unknown, null, case-alias, omitted-default, arbitrary-field, duplicate and collision mutations.

The focused .NET suite currently contains 29 cases; its canonical extraction admits 47 complete sources and emits eight exact closed dispatch roots.

The generated Lean file is theorem-free and does not import the handwritten transient-storage models. The separate refinement proves the generated TLOAD/TSTORE transition equal to `Eip803x.Evm.TransientStorageStack` after mapping the independently represented transient store into `Eip803x.Evm.TransientStorage`. It covers opcode bytes, fork admission, fixed warm-access gas, program-counter advance, gas exhaustion, stack underflow and TLOAD push behavior, TSTORE's static-before-charge rule, key-before-value pop order, and the successful write.

This is not a refinement of `IWorldState` or the whole EVM. Provider lookup and byte representation, transient-storage journaling, frame rollback/merge, `SELFDESTRUCT` interaction, transaction-boundary reset, world-state decorators, tracing callbacks, cancellation, CLR/JIT behavior, unsafe stack representation, and function-pointer execution remain explicit obligations in the generated IR. The trace-only pre-write byte-array snapshot for TSTORE is source-admitted but intentionally outside the consensus-state theorem.
