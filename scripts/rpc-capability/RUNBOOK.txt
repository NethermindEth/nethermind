is separate from Actions' concurrency lock. It survives job failure/cancellation.
Its owner binds run/attempt, exact private root and directory device/inode IDs.
A stale/foreign lock is never overwritten or removed. Current run private data is
rpc-private-<run>-<attempt>/ with mode0700 scratch/out/state. Fixed canonical

always-cleanup, no restore/finalize retry, no automatic archive/upload and no

UNKNOWN exits preserve the journal and private state. Root must establish helper
quiescence and recover exact owned resources before any further CI/workload.
GH Actions may release its concurrency slot on failure; the persistent reservation
and root's queue discipline remain in force. Host loss/kill cannot be converted
to success merely because a process disappeared.

encrypted/raw.tar.gz.cms, encrypted/archive.json, encrypted/recipient.crt,
